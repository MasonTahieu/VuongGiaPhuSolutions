# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Households in Đồng Xoài and across Đồng Nai** (primary): homeowners building or renovating a house or apartment. They need interior design and fit-out, or they are choosing and installing bathroom fixtures, water tanks, solar water heaters, electrical equipment, lighting or smart-home devices. They compare brands and want someone local they can trust to advise and install.
- **Owners and investors of projects** (primary): offices, rental rooms, hotels and other projects that need fit-out and equipment supplied and installed in larger quantities.
- **Businesses needing technology** (secondary): small companies and shops looking for custom software, digital transformation or applied AI.

## Product Purpose

The website of Vuong Gia Phu Solutions Company Limited (Vương Gia Phú). It presents two lines of business, interior/equipment and technology, plus the equipment brands the company supplies. Success means two things together:
1. a visitor trusts the business (a real, local, registered company that knows its brands), and
2. the visitor gets in touch: a phone call or Zalo message first, email or the Facebook fanpage second.

The website does not sell or take orders online.

## Positioning

- **One point of contact from advice to warranty:** consultation, design, supply, installation and after-sales support from a single local team in Đồng Xoài.
- **Genuine equipment from known brands:** INAX, TOTO, Viglacera, Caesar, Enic, Tân Á Đại Thành, Sơn Hà, Hoa Sen, Panasonic, Rạng Đông, Senko, Sino, Aqara, FPT Smart Home, Hunonic.
- **In-house technology team:** the same company builds software, which the site uses to back its smart-home work (e.g. designing automation scenarios).

Interior & equipment is the main business and main revenue; technology is the secondary line.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Pages:** 20 static pages, plus a 404 page.
  - Homepage, Công nghệ (technology), Nội thất (interior), Thiết bị & Thương hiệu (equipment catalogue in 5 categories), Chính sách bảo mật (privacy policy).
  - 15 brand pages under /thuong-hieu/.
- **Hosting:** static HTML/CSS/JS on GitHub Pages at vuonggiaphu.com, with no CMS. Pages are generated by tools/build_pages.py from the scraped original in site-goc/, using the redesigned homepage as the shared shell. tools/check_pages.py and tools/audit.py verify content and SEO.
- **Bilingual Vietnamese / English:** Vietnamese lives in the HTML; English lives in assets/js/i18n-en.js through data-i18n / data-en attributes. Any new text needs both languages.
- **Content and SEO must be preserved:** page text, URLs, titles, descriptions, canonical/hreflang tags and JSON-LD all stay the same. The privacy policy text is legal content and must stay word-for-word.
- **No online ordering function**, deliberately. Adding one would trigger notification duties under the 2025 E-commerce Law (online.gov.vn).
- **Must render reliably in iOS WebKit,** including in-app browsers. Avoid stacked blur, mask, filter or animated layers on touch screens, and avoid percentage-height images inside aspect-ratio boxes; both have blanked pages on real iPhones.
- **Undecided:** domain notification at thongbaotenmien.vn; enabling HTTPS on the live domain; a page for "rights and obligations / complaint handling".

## Product Principles

1. **Trust before persuasion:** show real, verifiable company facts and real brands; never invent proof.
2. **Contact is always one tap away:** phone and Zalo first, on every page and on phones.
3. **Interior & equipment lead; technology supports:** the main business gets the main attention.
4. **Local and concrete:** speak to people in Đồng Xoài / Đồng Nai about their house, their bathroom, their project.
5. **Works on the phone in the visitor's hand,** including iPhone in-app browsers, before it is impressive on a desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **Readers:** many are older homeowners on phones, so keep body text readable, tap targets comfortable, and contrast at WCAG AA.
- **Motion:** respect reduced-motion settings.
